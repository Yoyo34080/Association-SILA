# Les aventures de Zaid — Épisode : La forêt et les insectes
## مُغَامَرَاتُ زَيْدٍ — زَيْدٌ فِي الْغَابَةِ يَكْتَشِفُ الْحَشَرَاتِ

## Mode d'emploi
1. Joindre **l'image de référence de Zaid** à chaque génération (ou utiliser la fonction « référence de personnage » de l'outil : Midjourney `--cref`, Leonardo Character Reference, ChatGPT/DALL·E, Canva…).

## Scène 1 — L'arrivée dans la forêt
**Prompt :**
```
Zaid walks happily into a sunny green forest with tall trees, ferns, wildflowers and a small dirt path. He wears a small backpack and holds a magnifying glass, looking amazed. Wide establishing shot.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: مَا أَجْمَلَ الْغَابَةَ! هَيَّا نَبْحَثْ عَنِ الْحَشَرَاتِ!

*(Que la forêt est belle ! Allons chercher les insectes !)*

---

## Scène 2 — La fourmi (النَّمْلَةُ)
**Prompt :**
```
Zaid kneels on the forest floor, looking through his magnifying glass at a line of small ants; one ant carries a grain of wheat. Close-up, ants slightly enlarged and cute, curious expression on Zaid's face.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: اُنْظُرُوا! نَمْلَةٌ صَغِيرَةٌ تَحْمِلُ حَبَّةَ قَمْحٍ. النَّمْلَةُ قَوِيَّةٌ وَنَشِيطَةٌ!

*(Regardez ! Une petite fourmi porte un grain de blé. La fourmi est forte et travailleuse !)*

---

## Scène 3 — Le papillon (الْفَرَاشَةُ)
**Prompt :**
```
A big colorful butterfly with orange and blue wings flutters in front of Zaid in a sunny clearing full of flowers. Zaid smiles with wide eyes, hands open gently, not touching it.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: يَا لَهَا مِنْ فَرَاشَةٍ جَمِيلَةٍ! أَلْوَانُهَا زَاهِيَةٌ.

*(Quel joli papillon ! Ses couleurs sont éclatantes.)*

---

## Scène 4 — La coccinelle (الدُّعْسُوقَةُ)
**Prompt :**
```
A red ladybug with black dots sits on Zaid's raised index finger. Zaid counts the dots, smiling, with his other hand showing fingers. Close-up, soft blurred forest background.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: دُعْسُوقَةٌ حَمْرَاءُ عَلَى إِصْبَعِي! أَعُدُّ النِّقَاطَ: وَاحِدٌ، اثْنَانِ، ثَلَاثَةٌ...

*(Une coccinelle rouge sur mon doigt ! Je compte les points : un, deux, trois…)*

---

## Scène 5 — L'abeille (النَّحْلَةُ)
**Prompt :**
```
A cute bee collects nectar from a big yellow flower near a beehive hanging on a tree branch. Zaid watches from a safe distance, calm and fascinated, a small honey jar drawn as a thought bubble above him.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: النَّحْلَةُ تَجْمَعُ الرَّحِيقَ مِنَ الزُّهُورِ لِتَصْنَعَ الْعَسَلَ.

*(L'abeille récolte le nectar des fleurs pour fabriquer le miel.)*

---

## Scène 6 — La chenille (الْيَرَقَةُ)
**Prompt :**
```
A plump green caterpillar munches on a leaf on a low branch. Zaid leans in closely, pointing at it with a surprised and happy face. Small inset idea: a faint butterfly silhouette in the background.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: يَرَقَةٌ خَضْرَاءُ تَأْكُلُ وَرَقَةً. يَوْمًا مَا سَتُصْبِحُ فَرَاشَةً!

*(Une chenille verte mange une feuille. Un jour, elle deviendra un papillon !)*

---

## Scène 7 — La sauterelle (الْجُنْدُبُ)
**Prompt :**
```
A green grasshopper makes a big jump through tall grass; Zaid jumps too, imitating it, laughing, arms in the air. Dynamic action pose, motion lines, joyful mood.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: قَفْزَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ! إِنَّهُ جُنْدُبٌ أَخْضَرُ. أَنَا أَقْفِزُ مِثْلَهُ!

*(Un grand saut ! C'est une sauterelle verte. Je saute comme elle !)*

---

## Scène 8 — Les lucioles au coucher du soleil (الْيَرَاعَاتُ)
**Prompt :**
```
Golden sunset turning into dusk in the forest. Dozens of glowing fireflies float around Zaid, lighting his amazed face with soft yellow light. Magical, peaceful atmosphere.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: اُنْظُرُوا! الْيَرَاعَاتُ تُضِيءُ الْغَابَةَ.

*(Regardez ! Les lucioles illuminent la forêt.)*

---

## Scène 9 — Au revoir et morale
**Prompt :**
```
Zaid waves goodbye at the edge of the forest at dusk; the ant, butterfly, ladybug, bee, caterpillar, grasshopper and fireflies are gathered on a log and flowers, as if waving back. Warm, happy ending scene.
```
**Dialogue :**
> زَيْدٌ: مَعَ السَّلَامَةِ يَا أَصْدِقَائِي الصِّغَارَ! نَحْنُ نَحْتَرِمُ الْحَشَرَاتِ وَلَا نُؤْذِيهَا.

*(Au revoir mes petits amis ! Nous respectons les insectes et nous ne leur faisons pas de mal.)*
